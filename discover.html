Five essential topic areas with definitions, examples and data points for class notes, exams and projects.

01

Ecosystem

An ecosystem is the interaction of living (biotic) and non-living (abiotic) components. Assam has terrestrial forest ecosystems and aquatic systems connected to the Brahmaputra.

Example: Kaziranga is a grassland and wetland ecosystem.
02

Biodiversity

India is one of 17 mega-diverse countries and the Northeast is a biodiversity hotspot. Assam has five national parks, including Kaziranga, Manas, Nameri, Orang and Dibru-Saikhowa.

Kaziranga: 2,613 one-horned rhinos in the 2022 census.
03

Pollution

Air pollution in Guwahati is linked to vehicles and dust. Plastic and industrial waste affect the Brahmaputra, while excess fertilizer can affect soil health in tea-growing areas.

Study air, water and soil as connected systems.
04

Climate Change

Assam has experienced a long-term temperature rise, repeated floods and severe erosion. Majuli has lost a significant part of its area through erosion and climate pressures.

Floods can affect around 40% of Assam's land each year.
