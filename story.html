Regional case study

Environment of
Northeast India.

A region of forests, rivers, wetlands and living cultures, the Northeast shows how biodiversity, development and climate are connected.

01

Deforestation

The Northeast lost more than 3,000 sq km of forest cover between 2011 and 2021. Forest protection links directly to soil, water, wildlife and livelihoods.

02

Kaziranga

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and a major conservation success story, Kaziranga is home to around two-thirds of the world's one-horned rhinos.

03

Deepor Beel

Assam's Ramsar wetland near Guwahati supports birds and aquatic life but is threatened by garbage dumping, encroachment and urban pressure.

04

People's movements

Remember the Chipko Movement and Appiko Movement as important examples of community-led forest conservation and environmental action.
